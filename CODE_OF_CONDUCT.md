# Code of Conduct

This project adheres to the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private report via GitHub or contacting the maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
